About me

Curious by default,
dependable by habit

I'm Vaishnavi Goyal, a Computer Science undergraduate at PES University, Bengaluru — on scholarship, with a CGPA of 8.42.

What I've noticed about myself is that I'm happiest slightly out of my depth. Almost everything I've built started with me not knowing how to build it, and I've come to trust that feeling rather than avoid it. Learning quickly is the skill I rely on most, and it's the one that transfers to any team, tool or problem.

I'm steady to work with. I ask questions early instead of guessing, take feedback without taking it personally, and finish what I take on — even the unglamorous parts. When priorities shift or something breaks late, I'd rather adapt and keep things moving than wait for perfect clarity.

Outside of coursework I'm usually building something, and I care as much about doing it properly as getting it done — testing my own work, questioning my assumptions, and looking for what I might have missed.

How I work

What I'd bring to your team

I'm early in my career, so instead of a list of job titles, here's how I actually work day to day.

01 — Learning

I get up to speed fast

New language, new tool, new domain — I don't need a long runway. Most of what I've built began as something I had no idea how to do, and I've learned how to close that gap quickly and independently.

02 — Adaptability

I'm steady when things change

Requirements move, tools get swapped, plans fall through. I don't stall waiting for certainty — I reprioritise, ask what actually matters now, and keep the work moving forward.

03 — Ownership

I finish what I take on

If it's mine, it gets done — including the tedious last 10% that decides whether something actually works. You won't need to chase me for status or wonder where a task landed.

04 — Problem solving

I dig until I understand it

I'd rather know why something works than that it works. That habit means I fix root causes instead of symptoms, and I'm comfortable sitting with a hard problem long enough to actually solve it.

05 — Collaboration

I'm easy to work with

I ask questions early rather than guessing, share credit and context freely, and take feedback as information instead of criticism. Good work is rarely a solo effort, and I don't pretend otherwise.
