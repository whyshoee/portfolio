About me

A designer who thinks

Hi, I'm Vaishnavi Goyal — a UI/UX designer from Bengaluru focused on building human-centered digital products that solve real problems. I believe great design lives at the intersection of empathy, logic, and craft.

My process is rooted in understanding users deeply before touching a single frame — I research, synthesize, ideate, and iterate until the solution feels inevitable.

How I work

My design process

Every great product starts with understanding people, not pixels.

01 — Discover

Research & Empathize

I begin by deeply understanding users through interviews, surveys, and contextual inquiry. I map user journeys, identify pain points, and define the real problem — not just the assumed one.

02 — Define

Synthesize & Frame

Research becomes insights. I create personas, affinity maps, and "How might we…" statements that give design a clear purpose and align everyone on what we're solving.

03 — Ideate

Sketch & Conceptualize

Rapid ideation without judgment. Lo-fi sketches, crazy eights, storyboards — I generate many directions before narrowing down based on user needs and business goals.

04 — Prototype

Build & Refine

From wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes in Figma — interactive mockups that feel like the real thing, with component libraries and micro-interactions built in.

05 — Test

Validate & Iterate

Usability testing with real users surfaces what works and what doesn't. I measure, gather feedback, iterate fast, and never consider a design truly "done."
